BODOR GAME / DATA NOTICE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bodor Game handles information when you use this website. Bodor Game is an independent browser game project operated through the Szarazkoro/NeonCore project. For privacy questions, contact support@bodorgame.com.

Information we store

When you create an account, we store your callsign, a securely hashed password, game progression, arena level, credits and earned statistics. Before registration, a temporary guest profile such as PLAYER-AB12 is stored in the signed session cookie so the game can work without login. We do not store guest profiles in the database, and clearing the browser session removes that temporary progress. We do not store your plain-text password.

How information is used

Account and progression data is used to authenticate you, save your game state, operate the arena, display rankings and provide support when you contact us. We do not sell account information.

Cookies and local storage

Essential session cookies help keep you signed in and protect state-changing requests. The site also stores audio preferences in your browser's local storage. Google manages advertising consent and related advertising technologies through its configured Privacy & Messaging CMP. Where Google services are used, the CMP manages the relevant consent choices.

Advertising

We may display advertising or sponsorship placements on the website. Google AdSense may use advertising identifiers, including for personalized advertising where legally permitted and consented to. The Google Privacy & Messaging CMP presents the available consent choices, including accepting, refusing or managing settings.

Third-party services

Links to GitHub are provided for project information and contact. When you follow an external link, that service's own privacy policy applies. We do not control external websites. Google AdSense and its advertising partners may process data as described in Google's advertising technologies policy.

Your choices and requests

You can manage advertising consent through the Google CMP, clear your browser's stored site data, or contact us through the About / Contact page to ask about your account information or report a privacy concern.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service, advertising partners or applicable requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
