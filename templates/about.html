About the arena

Bodor Game is a browser-based cyber arena where short training modules turn practice into progression.

What is Bodor Game?

Train your fighter in focused mini-games, upgrade core stats, then take your build into the arena. The project is built as an independent game portal and is still evolving.

New modules, balancing changes and cosmetic features may arrive over time. Your account data is used to provide the game experience described in the Privacy Policy.
